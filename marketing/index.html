A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

Less mystery. More possibility.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. Webtummy gives you a visual editor to change your website’s text, images, colors, and sections without writing code.

What does the AI actually do?+

It uses your business brief to create a starting point: a website structure, a design direction, and draft content. You can then review and edit the result.

Can I change the website after creating it?+

Yes. Return to your workspace to open your project and keep editing. Adjust pages, replace images, update copy, and refine your design.

Can I manage more than one website?+

Webtummy organizes projects in a workspace. The number of websites you can create depends on your account’s plan.

How do I get started?+

Open the Webtummy builder, create your account, and tell us about your business. Your project begins with your idea and your brief.
